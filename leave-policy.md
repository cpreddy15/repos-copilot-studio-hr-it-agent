# Leave Policy (Sample — Fictional Company "Contoso")

## Annual Leave
Full-time employees receive **20 days** of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued monthly. Up to **5 unused days** may be carried forward to the next year.

## Sick Leave
Employees receive **10 days** of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than **2 consecutive days**.

## Casual Leave
**6 days** per year for personal matters, to be applied at least one day in advance where possible.

## Maternity & Paternity Leave
Maternity leave: **26 weeks** paid. Paternity leave: **2 weeks** paid, to be taken within 6 months of the child's birth.

## How to Apply
Submit a request through the Leave Request app. Your manager will receive an approval request in Teams and Outlook.
